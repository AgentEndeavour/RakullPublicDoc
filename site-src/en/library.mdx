## Bookshelf and filter

- Each book spine shows the **title, author and learning progress** (processed pages / total pages).
- A page being processed shows its live progress percentage.
- The **genre tabs** at the top filter the books already loaded.
- Pull to refresh or click **refresh** to reload the bookshelf.

On a phone, tap the already-selected **Deep Reading** item in the bottom navigation to switch between **Bookshelf / Weekly Recommendations** from the pop-up menu. On desktop both are always shown side by side.

## Preview and buy application books

**buy the book (CNY 9.90 by default)** or subscribe: **every active paid
subscription includes full reading access to all application books**.

A separately purchased book remains available after a subscription expires.
Other books return to preview mode. Access starts after the server confirms
payment and is revoked when that purchase is refunded. Administrators can adjust
prices and preview limits; check the displayed price before buying. Books you
upload continue to use the existing processing allowances and billing rules.

## Create a book

Click the **Upload** button to open the **Create book** wizard. Fill in the book details first, then choose the content type.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the book details">
    Set the title (required), author (optional), cover, genre and reading direction. Model, translation and explanation options belong to the later **Parsing settings** page, so no parsing setup is needed during upload.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the content type">
    Choose **Plain text** (paste Japanese text directly) or **Ebook** (upload a `.epub`, `.pdf` or `.txt` file).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automatic pagination">
    Choose **Simple / Standard / Advanced** analysis. Their default page caps are **300 / 500 / 800 characters**. Automatic mode uses a completed, non-degraded ability assessment, preferring its grammar dimension; unknown ability defaults to Standard.

    You can choose a maximum page length (100–1000 characters) on the upload screen. Changing difficulty preserves a manual cap. Counts use source Unicode characters, including punctuation and whitespace, rather than Japanese words or model tokens. Paragraph and sentence endings are preferred; an oversized sentence is split.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start learning">
    After upload and pagination, the book opens in an original-text preview without automatically processing any page. Select any page, click its processing button and confirm the charge to start processing. Choose the model, translation, explanations and output language on the dedicated parsing settings page for each page.

    **Simple** briefly explains more grammar; **Advanced** focuses on fewer difficult points. Pages keep the upload policy snapshot when parsed later, including retries.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After selecting Create, the upload form stays visible. A compact, reserved area above the actions shows fee estimation without inserting a large card or replacing the form. Content editing and Back are temporarily disabled so the quote matches the submitted source. Confirm the fee in the dialog; cancellation or preparation failure preserves your input and restores the form actions. Upload percentages describe file transfer only, not completion of processing. Phases without measurable progress use an indeterminate indicator, which stops while awaiting fee confirmation.

The creation dialog estimates the entire content, showing its total page count, character count and estimated total value, plus expected payment after current free and member allowances. It includes every page, per-page minimums and rounding; it neither prices only page one nor reuses one allowance for every page. This preview does not reserve the full amount. Actual page costs remain governed by page parsing quotes; creation, first-page handling and later parsing behavior are unchanged.

  The bookshelf stores paginated text. Original ebooks are retained temporarily for verification, extraction and recovery, then cleaned up after their retention period. They are not kept as permanent downloadable bookshelf files.

## Reading books: quick view

- Browse the original text of any page without processing it first. Turn through the whole book or tap the page number at the bottom to select a page directly.
- Every unprocessed page has the same status and **Tap to start processing** button. Select any page; earlier pages do not need to be processed. Processing starts after confirming the charge.
- Open a processed page for close reading: the source shows the whole book continuously and starts at the page you selected, without opening a separate reader for each page. Completing processing keeps the selected spread visible.
- On phones, swipe between the two pages. Existing long pages keep their page numbers and results, with readable, scrollable text.


**Processed pages keep their text at full contrast** and open close reading. **Unparsed pages show faded text**, with the processing prompt kept clear above it, so reading and parsing are easy to distinguish. Locked pages also use the faded preview.

In close reading, continue through the full source and select sentences on other pages. Processed pages show their corresponding translations and grammar explanations; audio, saved sentences and notes also belong to the selected sentence. Unparsed pages keep their original text and show an explicit unparsed status when selected. Browsing, scrolling or selecting unparsed content does not start processing or incur processing charges.

Click **Reset parsing** at the top right of a processed page and review the confirmation dialog. Confirming returns that page to its unparsed state while retaining the original text, favorites and learning history. Cancel leaves it unchanged. Click the page again to start parsing and confirm its cost through the usual flow. Reset is unavailable while any page in the book is being processed. Resetting an earlier page brings it into view while keeping later processed pages accessible.

## Parsing settings

Tap the processing button on any unprocessed page to open a dedicated screen:

- **LLM polish**: choose the model, explanation mode, explanation difficulty (adapt to your ability by default), and output language.
- **Lite translation**: choose an available translation backend and output language. Model and grammar controls are hidden.
- **Reading self-checks**: set the selected page’s self-check option. Enabling checks still requires a completed ability assessment.
- **Charge**: the estimate uses the current options. Changing an option invalidates the old estimate; review a new estimate before starting.

Existing books prefill their saved options. Settings apply only to the selected page. Back or Cancel starts no parsing. Accepted tasks resume with the original options and request identity to avoid duplicate charges.

## Play with Raku while waiting

Uploads, book-page parsing, article reparsing, and processing after source edits share a full parsing page with a game available during submission and queueing. Parsing progress no longer uses a dialog. When reopening an article that is still processing, use the action in its progress area to open the same page and follow the existing task without submitting it again. Completion returns to refreshed content; parsing failures appear on the page. Charge and option confirmations retain their existing flow, and furigana generation is unchanged.

While your article is processing, tap or press space to start; hold to jump higher. Collect a **★ +5** target word for 5 points and a visible shield that blocks one obstacle collision. Shields do not stack or expire. A collision consumes the shield; damage and restarting never grant one. White words award no points, and obstacles keep spawning normally.

Controls and reward rules remain visible below the track, clear of Raku, before and during play. Damage uses a smaller body-core hitbox, allowing more forgiving grazes around the hat and clothing. Reward collection keeps its wider range. When all lives are lost, a large 菜 stamp appears; dismiss it to restart.

Airborne spikes leave a clear gap above Raku when standing, so staying grounded lets you pass underneath. Jumping into a spike costs a life. Damage does not grant protection; collect a target word to earn a shield.

## Process multiple book pages continuously

Enable **Process book pages continuously** during upload to open **Batch parsing settings** immediately after pagination. Browse original pages and use the same model, translation, explanation, language and self-check controls as single-page parsing. Confirm to create a background queue that processes this book’s pending pages in page order. Without this option, upload opens the original preview as usual. The preview’s **Batch process** action can process the current page and all later pending pages at any time.

**Each user can process only one book at a time.** The confirmed queue holds the processing slot until it finishes or is stopped. Each completed page becomes readable immediately while later pages continue, including after leaving preview, entering the reader or closing the browser. The preview’s **Task queue** action has a black badge counting pending and active pages. Open it to inspect page statuses and errors or read completed pages. It also includes ordinary single-page tasks and pages that have not been queued.

Estimates use current allowances. Creating a queue does not reserve payment for every page; each page checks its confirmed quote when it starts. Insufficient balance or changed allowances/funding pause the queue without switching automatically to wallet payment. Ordinary page failures are recorded and later pages continue. Cancel one task from its row or all tasks from the queue footer. The processing slot remains occupied until the server confirms execution has ended. Batch processing skips completed pages; use the dedicated row action to reparse a completed page. Confirm estimates within 10 minutes. Per-page queue quotes remain valid for up to 7 days and require review after expiry.

Deployment requires the durable processing schema, corresponding workers and the page-processing V2 gate. Environments without these do not accept queue requests.

Active pages show a progress bar: valid worker counts display the current phase percentage and completed/total count. Without valid counts, an indeterminate bar indicates activity without inventing a percentage. Queued, failed and completed pages do not show an active progress bar.

Click any page entry to open live task details with status, phase progress, errors and the original page; readable results have a reading action in details. Completed pages show only **Reparse page**. Waiting pages have no row actions. Active task actions remain on the right. These buttons do not open task details. Cancellation waits for the server’s final state and shows “Stopping” while execution winds down. Reparse opens settings and cost review, then waits for the old task to stop safely before starting. Completed results remain available until a successful replacement is published; cancellation or failure preserves them. **Cancel all remaining tasks** at the bottom cancels waiting pages and requests cancellation of current execution. A page already committing its final result may finish saving first. Each user still processes only one book at a time.

## Recoverable uploads (internal preview)

When an upload enters the server queue, it shows “Queued, currently number N”. The position updates as tasks are claimed and scheduling priorities change; execution replaces it with the current processing stage. If the server cannot determine a reliable position, the app only shows “Queued”. Waiting no longer displays a seconds-since-progress warning. Connection problems and processing failures are still reported.

The position is a snapshot of the currently eligible queue, not a waiting-time estimate. When processing engines compete for the same execution slot, a retry is pending, or execution is not yet eligible, only the queue label may be available.

Preview versions show upload, preparation, fee confirmation and processing progress. Confirming the fee after preparation creates the book and processes its first page. Quotes reuse the uploaded source, so the file does not need to be uploaded again.

Accepted tasks continue after leaving the page or losing the network. Reopen Create book to restore the session. After a browser refresh, reselect the original file if requested; the app verifies its identity before resuming. Retries keep the same request to avoid duplicate books. Signing out isolates your recovery records from other accounts.

A connection problem does not mark background work as failed. Reconnect to check its state. If the task requires attention or reconciliation, keep its task ID and contact support before creating another import.

## Cancel page processing

A page can be cancelled while queued or processing with **Cancel processing** in the progress area. Only the task owner and root can cancel.

After confirmation, the button shows **Cancelling…** while waiting for the server's final state. Generated text, explanations and sentence audio are preserved; a page that failed stays in the failed state and can be retried later by tapping it again. An upload session that has not been submitted for processing expires after two minutes without activity and its temporary files are cleaned up. Deleting a book also removes the page content it has already generated.

Choose whether to enable reading self-checks before processing; they are off by default. Set the choice in the new-book processing form or the next-page confirmation in quick view. A completed ability assessment is required. The choice is saved with the content, and the translation pane has no switch. Without checks, the original translation and full explanation are shown directly. With checks, Threads-style particles reveal hidden content on tap instead of a collapsible full-explanation section.

With reading self-checks enabled, occasional translation fragments may appear as stars. Tap the sentence to reveal them. Each reading-page session allows at most two checks, one per paragraph and at least five sentences apart. The rest of the translation stays readable. Turning self-checks off shows translations and explanations in full.

## Choose a book cover

The first creation step selects a random cover. You can choose charcoal leather, graphite parchment, brown leather, or warm parchment, with a live preview of the title and author. The choice survives moving between steps and retrying creation.

Both plain text and e-book uploads support cover selection. After creation, the home shelf uses the saved cover across refreshes and devices. Older books without a saved cover receive a stable cover based on their book ID.

## Manage my articles

After signing in, **My articles** beside **Upload** opens your personal management page. It lists only your uploads, including processing records. Search by title or author, check status, open articles, or delete them. Public reading material and site-wide admin listings are separate. Wait for processing to finish, or cancel it, before deleting an active article. Group recommendations use the same personal list and show only articles ready to read.

Deleting a book also deletes its exclusively owned backing articles. If deletion fails, the book and articles remain intact so you can retry. Historical remnants of deleted books are excluded from ordinary article lists and reading access.
