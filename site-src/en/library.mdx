After logging in you land on **Deep Reading** by default. Your learning books live on a **bookshelf**: each book stands upright on a shelf and shows its title, author and learning progress. On desktop, **Weekly Recommendations** are shown on the right. Click a book to open it.

## Bookshelf and filter

- Each book spine shows the **title, author and learning progress** (processed pages / total pages).
- A page being processed shows its live progress percentage.
- The **genre tabs** at the top filter the books already loaded.
- Pull to refresh or click **refresh** to reload the bookshelf.

On a phone, tap the already-selected **Deep Reading** item in the bottom navigation to switch between **Bookshelf / Weekly Recommendations** from the pop-up menu. On desktop both are always shown side by side.

## Preview and buy application books

Kanji, JLPT Grammar and Raku Grammar offer a free preview. To read beyond it,
**buy the book (CNY 9.90 by default)** or subscribe: **every active paid
subscription includes full reading access to all application books**.

A separately purchased book remains available after a subscription expires.
Other books return to preview mode. Access starts after the server confirms
payment and is revoked when that purchase is refunded. Administrators can adjust
prices and preview limits; check the displayed price before buying. Books you
upload continue to use the existing processing allowances and billing rules.

## Create a book

Click the **Upload** button to open the **Create book** wizard. Fill in the book details first, then choose the content type.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the book details">
    Set the title (required), author (optional), genre and translation type. **Lite translation** uses machine translation without a model summary or grammar explanations; you can select a translation backend. **LLM polish** offers explanation mode and model options; explanation depth adapts to observed abilities without selecting N1–N5. Both types support an output language (default Simplified Chinese).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the content type">
    Choose **Plain text** (paste Japanese text directly) or **Ebook** (upload a `.epub`, `.pdf` or `.txt` file).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automatic pagination">
    The content is paginated by paragraph: **each page holds at most 300 characters (including punctuation, excluding whitespace)**, ending at a paragraph/sentence boundary when possible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start learning">
    After upload and pagination, the book opens in an original-text preview without automatically processing any page. Select any page, click its processing button and confirm the charge to start processing. Every page uses the translation type and backend selected at creation; lite translation does not generate grammar explanations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After selecting Create, the upload form stays visible. A compact, reserved area above the actions shows fee estimation without inserting a large card or replacing the form. Content editing and Back are temporarily disabled so the quote matches the submitted source. Confirm the fee in the dialog; cancellation or preparation failure preserves your input and restores the form actions. Upload percentages describe file transfer only, not completion of processing. Phases without measurable progress use an indeterminate indicator, which stops while awaiting fee confirmation.

The creation dialog estimates the entire content, showing its total page count, character count and estimated total value, plus expected payment after current free and member allowances. It includes every page, per-page minimums and rounding; it neither prices only page one nor reuses one allowance for every page. This preview does not reserve the full amount. Actual page costs remain governed by page parsing quotes; creation, first-page handling and later parsing behavior are unchanged.

<Note>
  The bookshelf stores paginated text. Original ebooks are retained temporarily for verification, extraction and recovery, then cleaned up after their retention period. They are not kept as permanent downloadable bookshelf files.
</Note>

<Tip>
  The three genres are split differently: **Article / Interview** are split on sentence-ending punctuation, while **Lyrics** are split line by line, so separate each lyric line with a line break. Scanned / image-only PDFs cannot be extracted and are not supported.
</Tip>

## Reading books: quick view

- Browse the original text of any page without processing it first. Turn through the whole book or tap the page number at the bottom to select a page directly.
- Every unprocessed page has the same status and **Tap to start processing** button. Select any page; earlier pages do not need to be processed. Processing starts after confirming the charge.
- Open processed pages for close reading, translations, explanations and audio. Completing processing keeps the selected spread visible.
- On phones, swipe between the two pages. Existing long pages keep their page numbers and results, with readable, scrollable text.


**Processed pages keep their text at full contrast** and open close reading. **Unparsed pages show faded text**, with the processing prompt kept clear above it, so reading and parsing are easy to distinguish. Locked pages also use the faded preview.

Click **Reset parsing** at the top right of a processed page and review the confirmation dialog. Confirming returns that page to its unparsed state while retaining the original text, favorites and learning history. Cancel leaves it unchanged. Click the page again to start parsing and confirm its cost through the usual flow. Reset is unavailable while any page in the book is being processed. Resetting an earlier page brings it into view while keeping later processed pages accessible.

## Play with Raku while waiting

While your article is processing, tap or press space to start the runner game; hold to jump higher. A filled word capsule marked **★ +5** is a reward: collect it for 5 points and a six-second bonus. During the bonus, airborne spikes disappear, while ground obstacles keep appearing and still award points when passed. White outlined word capsules show **+1** and award one point each when collected. After the bonus ends, airborne spikes return and white capsules stop awarding points. The game displays your score and point feedback on collection.

Airborne spikes leave a clear gap above Raku when standing, so staying grounded lets you pass underneath. Jumping into a spike costs a life. After a hit, Raku flashes and shows a shield for 1.2 seconds; collisions during this brief protection do not cost another life.

## Recoverable uploads (internal preview)

Preview versions show upload, preparation, fee confirmation and processing progress. Confirming the fee after preparation creates the book and processes its first page. Quotes reuse the uploaded source, so the file does not need to be uploaded again.

Accepted tasks continue after leaving the page or losing the network. Reopen Create book to restore the session. After a browser refresh, reselect the original file if requested; the app verifies its identity before resuming. Retries keep the same request to avoid duplicate books. Signing out isolates your recovery records from other accounts.

A connection problem does not mark background work as failed. Reconnect to check its state. If the task requires attention or reconciliation, keep its task ID and contact support before creating another import.

## Cancel page processing

A page can be cancelled while queued or processing with **Cancel processing** in the progress area. Only the task owner and root can cancel.

After confirmation, the button shows **Cancelling…** while waiting for the server's final state. Generated text, explanations and sentence audio are preserved; a page that failed stays in the failed state and can be retried later by tapping it again. An upload session that has not been submitted for processing expires after two minutes without activity and its temporary files are cleaned up. Deleting a book also removes the page content it has already generated.

Choose whether to enable reading self-checks before processing; they are off by default. Set the choice in the new-book processing form or the next-page confirmation in quick view. A completed ability assessment is required. The choice is saved with the content, and the translation pane has no switch. Without checks, the original translation and full explanation are shown directly. With checks, Threads-style particles reveal hidden content on tap instead of a collapsible full-explanation section.

With reading self-checks enabled, occasional translation fragments may appear as stars. Tap the sentence to reveal them. Each reading-page session allows at most two checks, one per paragraph and at least five sentences apart. The rest of the translation stays readable. Turning self-checks off shows translations and explanations in full.

## Choose a book cover

The first creation step selects a random cover. You can choose charcoal leather, graphite parchment, brown leather, or warm parchment, with a live preview of the title and author. The choice survives moving between steps and retrying creation.

Both plain text and e-book uploads support cover selection. After creation, the home shelf uses the saved cover across refreshes and devices. Older books without a saved cover receive a stable cover based on their book ID.
