The **Interest Plaza** tab at the far right of the top navigation is where you discover and join **interest groups**. Guests can browse the plaza freely; joining and creating groups require an account.

On phones, search, the + button for creating a group, and More stay on one row. More contains the share-code join and group-management actions.

## Browse and filter groups

The plaza lists every **active, public** group as a card. Each card shows:

- The **group name** and **description**
- **Tags** (e.g. anime, music)
- The **member count** and **featured article count**
- A preview of featured article titles

Use the **search box** to find groups by name, or the **tag filter bar** to see only groups for a specific interest. Pull down to refresh.

> **Private groups never appear on the plaza** — non-members cannot search for them or open their detail pages. Only members can see them (see [Public vs private](#public-vs-private-groups)).

## Joining a group

There are two ways:

1. **Join by share code**: get the group's share code from a member and choose "Join by code". You become a member immediately, no approval needed.
2. **Request to join**: open the group detail page and choose "Request to join", optionally with a message. The group Owner or an admin must approve your request. While waiting, the page shows a pending status.

## Creating a group

Anyone can choose "Create a group" and submit an application with:

- A **group name** (2–40 characters, unique across the site)
- A **description**
- The **main interest**
- **1–6 tags**
- A description of the **planned content**
- **Visibility**: public (default) or private

The group goes live only after a site administrator approves it. If your application is rejected, you can see the reason under "My applications".

## Public vs private groups

- **Public (default)**: listed on the Interest Plaza; anyone can browse and request to join.
- **Private**: absent from the plaza, search, and tag filters. Non-members cannot tell the group exists or request to join; new members can only be added directly with the group's **share code**.
- Either way, a group always appears in its **members'** own group list and in the home "Interest picks" panel — visibility only affects public plaza exposure, never what members see.
- Owners/admins can switch between public and private at any time on the [group management](#managing-your-groups) page. The change takes effect immediately and never affects existing members.

## Official weekly history

The **Official weekly history** block at the top of the plaza shows the full history of weekly picks (both currently featured and removed ones) in a horizontal row. Tap a card to open the article.

## Group details and recommendations

Group details and home **Interest picks** share two content cards:

- **Excerpt**: the selected original text, its translation and a recommendation note.
- **Grammar**: the grammar form, full explanation and examples, with associated original text and translation where applicable.

Book/article titles and page numbers are attribution metadata. Recommendations do not display whole books or grant access to the source or its other pages.

### Recommend an excerpt or grammar

Choose **Choose an excerpt or grammar** in the group reading section. Select a continuous saved sentence range from your own processed content, a grammar occurrence from a single sentence, or a public grammar card. The reader offers the same recommendation menu for your selected sentence and its matched grammar. Grammar browsing and detail views also provide the shared entry. Personal entitlement to protected reference material does not make it publicly shareable across groups.

The server builds a complete preview before submission. Choose a group, optionally add a note, and submit for group review followed by site review. **Both reviewers see the complete content, note, attribution and expandable bilingual context—the same immutable snapshot that members will read.**

Expand **Context: original and translation** to view the related saved paragraphs. Context is limited to the recommendation, rather than copying an entire book. Reparsing or editing the original never silently changes an approved snapshot. If source content changes after preview, refresh the preview before submitting; a new version needs review. Deleting the source does not erase a published snapshot or grant access to deleted source content.

Legacy article recommendations are retained as history and are never automatically converted into excerpts. The new recommendation feed shows only excerpts and grammar.

## Managing your group

Group details show direct destinations and pending counts. **Manage my groups** on the plaza opens the same destinations.

- **Recommendation review**: owners/admins see group review, site review and reviewed entries. Inspect the original, translation and context before sending an item to site review. Regular members see only their own submissions.
- **Members**: handle join requests and view members; owners additionally handle admin requests. Regular members can leave.
- **Group settings**: manage visibility and invitation codes. Only the owner can confirm dissolution.

> The site console lists recommendations that have passed group review. Leaving, removal or dissolution removes that group's grant to the recommendation snapshots.

## Members and large groups

The common header keeps the title, introduction, tags, and invitation actions visible across content changes. Liquid Glass tabs sit below this header and switch only recommendations, members, and management. The member tab shows the group size directly. Only members can open the full directory; visitors see a join prompt.

The directory uses server-side cursor pagination with 25 people per request, role filters, and exact member-ID lookup. Only the current page is fetched, name-resolved, and rendered. Returning to the directory preserves its current query. Management also uses tabs for overview, review, members, and settings.
