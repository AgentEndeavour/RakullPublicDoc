Raku is an **immersive Japanese reading platform**. Upload an article or a video and the app automatically splits it into sentences, translates it, and generates grammar explanations and audio narration. You read sentence by sentence in the reader and save sentences and words as reviewable cards.

This guide only covers **how to use the app's interface**; it does not cover installation or deployment.

## First launch

When you open the app you see a welcome screen with the app name **Raku Learning Language** in the middle. Click **Login** to continue.

## Log in

For an existing account, check “**I have read and agree to the Privacy Policy**” on the login screen, then choose “**Continue with Google**” or “**Continue with GitHub**” and select your account on the authorization page. Click “Privacy Policy” to read the full policy.

The login screen has no invite-code field and signing in does not require an invite code. After logging in you land on the main screen. The app **remembers your session**, so next time you open it you skip the login screen. You can **log out** from the account menu in the top-right corner.

## Register a new account

Click “**No account? Create one**” on the login screen to open registration:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter an invite code">
    Enter a valid **invite code**, available from an administrator or a user with a personal invite code. Invite codes are only entered during registration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agree to the privacy policy">
    Check “**I have read and agree to the Privacy Policy**”.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a registration provider">
    Click “**Register with Google**” or “**Register with GitHub**” and complete authorization with your provider account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A valid invite code completes registration immediately and opens the main screen. An invalid code shows an error on the registration screen; correct it and click “**Register**” to retry using the same authorization.

<Note>
  If an unregistered account completes authorization through the login entry, it first sees “**This account is not registered yet.**”. Click “**No account? Create one**”, enter an invite code, agree to the privacy policy, and click “**Register**” to finish without another authorization redirect.
</Note>

Click “**Have an account? Sign in**” on the registration screen to return to login.

## Browse without an account

At the bottom of the login screen there is a “**Browse without logging in**” button. Click it to enter the app as a **guest**: you can browse public corpus and view existing translations, explanations, and grammar cards, and play any audio that already exists.

Guests **cannot** upload content, favorite articles or sentences, or add words to their vocabulary; these actions prompt you to log in.

## Switch the interface language

In the **top-right corner** of any page, click the language button (it shows the current language with a translate icon) to switch between **English / 简体中文 / 繁體中文 / 日本語**. Your choice is remembered for next time.

<Note>
  This switches the **interface language**; article translations and grammar explanations are generated by the backend and do not change with the interface language.
</Note>
