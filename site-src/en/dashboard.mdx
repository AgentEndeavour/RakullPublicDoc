The **Dashboard** tab is your **learning overview**, aggregating the activity data under your account.

</Frame>

## Statistic cards

The cards at the top show the various counts:

- **Articles**: the number of articles you own.
- **Sentences**: the total number of sentences (excluding blank layout lines) in your articles.
- **Learned sentences**: how many sentences have explanations and have been read.
- **Collections / saved cards**: the number of collections and the sentence cards within them.
- **Uploads / LLM calls**: upload counts and AI call statistics.

## Learning progress

The circular progress chart at the bottom-left shows the **percentage of learned sentences out of all sentences** (e.g. 100%, 16/16), so you can see overall completion at a glance.

## Activity statistics

The horizontal bar chart at the bottom-right places the counts side by side (articles, sentences, learned sentences, collections, saved cards, uploads, LLM calls) for easy comparison.

## Your donations

There is also a “**Your donations**” area showing your past donations. You can support the project through “Donate” and view the list of supporters in “Thank you to our supporters”.

## Membership and wallet

Membership is grouped into plan status, current allowance, and unlocked articles. Paid members see expiry and reset dates; length-based plans show separate short, medium, and long balances. Unlocked articles count distinct successfully parsed articles across free, membership, and wallet use. Retranslations, failed tasks, and pending work do not add articles. Open a recent article or choose View all to page through history. Deleted or inaccessible articles are unavailable; history never grants access.

**My benefits** in the account center shows your free or current membership,
remaining allowance and expiry. **View benefits** opens the current plan’s benefits,
quota rules, and billing explanation. Choose **Get a membership** or **Renew membership**
to open plan selection, then choose a plan, monthly/quarterly/yearly period and an
available payment method. If your wallet has insufficient funds, top up first and
confirm the purchase again after returning.

For wallet top-ups, enter an amount and review the expected credits. No fixed
package is required. Card payment is selected first when available; **Top up**
opens the hosted card checkout. Other enabled methods remain available. If the
browser blocks automatic opening, use the button in the payment dialog to reopen
the same order. Closing or cancelling payment does not confirm a top-up: balance
and membership changes depend on the server-confirmed payment result.

Use **Order history** for purchase and top-up orders, payment status, continuing payment, and after-sales requests. The wallet keeps balance and top-up controls. **Credit activity** is collapsed by default; open it to review top-ups, spending, refunds, holds, and releases.
