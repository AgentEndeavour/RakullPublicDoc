The **Favorites** tab in the main navigation brings together your learning outcomes and splits them into **Words** and **Sentences**, which are independent of each other.

<Frame caption="Favorites: the Words and Sentences entries">
  <img src="/images/user-guide/13-favorites.png" alt="Favorites home" />
</Frame>

## Favorite whole articles

Once logged in, click the **bookmark** on cards in the library or weekly recommendations, or in the reader's top toolbar, to favorite a whole article. An outlined bookmark means not yet favorited, a filled bookmark means favorited; click again to remove it.

Favoriting a whole article is for reading it later; it is independent of sentence cards and vocabulary.

## Words (vocabulary)

Look up a word in the reader and click “**Add to vocabulary**” on the word card to add it to your vocabulary. The top shows today's plan, the total number of saved words, and how many you have learned.

Vocabulary has two modes:

- **Memory mode**: the spaced repetition algorithm schedules the words due today; click “start memorizing” to go through them one by one.
- **Quick lookup**: view all your words in a list, filter by category or tag, or search.

<Frame caption="Quick lookup: form, reading, and meaning">
  <img src="/images/user-guide/14-vocabulary.png" alt="Vocabulary quick lookup" />
</Frame>

Each entry records the form, reading, part of speech, meaning, and example source; you can also add words manually.

## Sentences (sentence cards)

Select a sentence in the reader and click “**Save this sentence**” in the source module. Choose an existing collection from the pop-up list or create a new one, and the sentence is saved as a card.

The sentences page is organized by collection: “All” gathers every sentence card, and there is an entry to create a new collection.

<Frame caption="Sentence collections: all, your own collections, and create">
  <img src="/images/user-guide/15-sentence-collections.png" alt="Sentence collections" />
</Frame>

<Note>
  A sentence card keeps the **snapshot** from when it was saved (Japanese on the front, translation on the back). Even if the original is later edited or deleted, the card stays the same.
</Note>

## Spaced repetition review

Open any collection and click “start review” to enter the card browser:

- The **front** first shows only the Japanese sentence; click the card or “view translation”.
- After flipping to the **back**, compare it with the translation and rate yourself.

<Frame caption="Card front: see the Japanese first and actively recall">
  <img src="/images/user-guide/16-sentence-cards.png" alt="Sentence card front" />
</Frame>

<Frame caption="Card back: the four ratings decide the next review time">
  <img src="/images/user-guide/17-sentence-card-back.png" alt="Sentence card back with ratings" />
</Frame>

The four ratings map to different intervals:

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Again** | Appears again very soon (e.g. about 1 minute) |
| **Hard** | A shorter interval (e.g. about 10 minutes) |
| **Good** | The normal interval (e.g. about 1 day) |
| **Easy** | A longer interval (e.g. about 4 days) |

The algorithm schedules the next review based on your rating, showing each card only as you are about to forget it, so you remember with the fewest repetitions.
